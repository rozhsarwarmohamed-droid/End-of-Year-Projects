📋 These projects count toward the final 10 project marks. They are designed to be simple, short, and suitable for Grade 3 students.

Subject Projects

English
Helper Robot
Poster
🤖

The Task

Each student designs a helper robot and prepares a small poster, then gives a 1-minute presentation.

Poster Must Include
  • A drawing of the robot
  • The robot's name
  • 5 sentences about what the robot can do
  • 3 instructions for using the robot

🏠 At Home

  • Draw the robot
  • Write the first draft

🏫 At School

  • Finalize the poster
  • Practise speaking
  • Present the project

🎨 Materials Needed

  • Paper or card
  • Pencils & coloured pencils
Marking — Out of 10
3
Ideas are clear and suitable
3
Sentences are understandable and mostly correct
2
Instructions make sense
2
Student explains the robot clearly
Mathematics
3D Shape
Museum
🔷

The Task

Each student builds one 3D shape and prepares a small fact card, then gives a 1-minute explanation.

Project Must Include
  • The 3D model
  • The name of the shape
  • Number of faces, edges & vertices
  • 1 sentence comparing it to another shape

🏠 At Home

  • Cut & fold the net
  • Glue the model

🏫 At School

  • Label the shape
  • Complete the fact card
  • Present the project

🎨 Materials Needed

  • Printed net · scissors · glue
  • Pencil & coloured pencils
Marking — Out of 10
4
Shape is built correctly
2
Facts (faces, edges, vertices) are correct
2
Comparison sentence makes sense
1

1
Oral explanation
Science
Sun, Earth &
Moon Model
🌍

The Task

Each student makes a moving Sun–Earth–Moon model and gives a 1-minute explanation of why the Moon seems to change shape.

Project Must Include
  • A labelled model (Sun, Earth, Moon)
  • 3 facts about the system
  • Explanation of why the Moon appears to change shape

🏠 At Home

  • Colour & cut model pieces
  • Learn the 3 facts

🏫 At School

  • Assemble the model
  • Label the model
  • Present the explanation

🎨 Materials Needed

  • Printed template or card circles
  • Scissors · split pins · coloured pencils
Marking — Out of 10
3
Model is complete and labelled correctly
3
Science facts are correct
2
Student explains Moon movement or phases clearly
2
Neatness and effort
